02 / Overview

A question in Teams. An answer from the warehouse.

Ramanujan is an analytics copilot that lives where business teams already talk: Microsoft Teams. Someone asks a question in plain English, and a Gemini agent turns it into governed BigQuery SQL, runs it and replies with the answer, without the person writing SQL or opening a dashboard.

  1. Ask
  2. Ground
  3. Generate
  4. Guard
  5. Answer

Ramanujan / how the parts connect

03 / The problem

Every simple question waited on someone who could write the query.

Business teams had questions about their numbers every day, but the answers sat in a data warehouse behind SQL and dashboards. Each question became a request to the data team, and the same metric could be calculated differently depending on who wrote the query.

  1. 01

    Answers locked behind SQL and dashboards

  2. 02

    Metrics defined differently by different people

  3. 03

    Data access that has to respect who is asking

04 / How it works

Five steps from a sentence to a governed answer.

  1. 01

    Ask

    A question arrives from Microsoft Teams through the Microsoft Graph API.

  2. 02

    Ground

    RAG over a KPI glossary in Qdrant finds the business definition of each metric.

  3. 03

    Generate

    A Gemini ReAct agent writes BigQuery SQL that follows those definitions.

  4. 04

    Guard

    A SQL policy engine checks the query and applies row-level access control.

  5. 05

    Answer

    The result comes back to Teams, grounded in the data, not guessed.

05 / Key decisions

Where the important calls were made.

Decision 01

Should the model be free to query anything?

  1. →

    The choice

    Every query passes a SQL policy engine with row-level access control.

  2. →

    What it gave

    People only ever see the data they are allowed to see.

Decision 02

How do answers match what the business means by a metric?

  1. →

    The choice

    RAG over a KPI glossary with sentence-transformer embeddings.

  2. →

    What it gave

    GMV, margin or fill rate mean the same thing in every answer.

Decision 03

How should the service be structured?

  1. →

    The choice

    A FastAPI service on Vertex AI built with hexagonal architecture.

  2. →

    What it gave

    Models, data sources and chat channels can be swapped without touching the core.
