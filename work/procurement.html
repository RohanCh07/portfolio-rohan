02 / Overview

Sourcing, in the chat app everyone already has.

The procurement assistant is an AI agent reachable on WhatsApp. People describe what they need in their own words, and the agent works out the request, runs the right sourcing workflow through backend services and replies in the same conversation.

  1. Message
  2. Understand
  3. Act
  4. Reply

Conversational Procurement / how the parts connect

03 / The problem

Sourcing meant forms, portals and follow-up calls.

Starting a sourcing request usually meant logging in to a portal, filling a form and chasing updates. Many of the people who needed it were on the move and already lived in WhatsApp.

  1. 01

    A portal to log in to for every request

  2. 02

    Forms that do not fit how people ask

  3. 03

    Updates scattered across calls and emails

04 / How it works

Four steps from a message to a sourcing action.

  1. 01

    Message

    A user writes to the assistant on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Understand

    The agent works out what is being asked and what is missing.

  3. 03

    Act

    Agent orchestration calls the sourcing workflows through backend REST APIs.

  4. 04

    Reply

    A contextual answer comes back in the same conversation.

05 / Key decisions

Where the important calls were made.

Decision 01

Why WhatsApp?

  1. →

    The choice

    Meet people in the app they already use every day.

  2. →

    What it gave

    No new tool to learn and no portal to log in to.

Decision 02

A fixed menu or an agent?

  1. →

    The choice

    An agent that understands free text and asks for what is missing.

  2. →

    What it gave

    People can ask the way they would ask a colleague.

Decision 03

Where does the logic live?

  1. →

    The choice

    Backend services the agent orchestrates, not the chat itself.

  2. →

    What it gave

    Workflows stay consistent whichever channel starts them.
