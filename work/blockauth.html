02 / Overview

Identity and access for IoT devices, without a single point of trust.

BlockAuth is a blockchain-based decentralised identity management system for secure authentication and access control across IoT networks. The research was presented at ICSDS-2025, Hooghly Engineering & Technology College, in December 2025.

BlockAuth / how the parts connect

03 / The problem

IoT networks usually trust one central server to say who is who.

When identity and access rely on a single authority, that authority becomes a bottleneck and a target. Large fleets of small devices need a way to authenticate and control access that does not depend on one server staying up and uncompromised.

  1. 01

    A single point of failure for identity

  2. 02

    A central target for attackers

  3. 03

    Hard to scale across many small devices

04 / How it works

Identity, permissions and checks recorded on a shared ledger.

  1. 01

    Register

    Each device gets a decentralised identity on the chain.

  2. 02

    Grant

    Access permissions are recorded as transactions.

  3. 03

    Authenticate

    Devices prove their identity against the ledger.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Any participant can check access without a central server.

05 / Key decisions

Where the important calls were made.

Decision 01

Where should identity live?

  1. →

    The choice

    On a blockchain, not a central server.

  2. →

    What it gave

    No single authority to fail or attack.

Decision 02

How is access controlled?

  1. →

    The choice

    Permissions recorded on the same ledger.

  2. →

    What it gave

    Access decisions are transparent and verifiable by every participant.
