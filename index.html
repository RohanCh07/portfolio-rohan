Case notes

Lumida connects read-only to any database and maps it out: the relationships nobody documented, what "normal" looks like for each table, and where the data has drifted. Every finding comes with evidence and a plain-language explanation from an LLM, and it learns from people marking findings as real or not.

On a test database with planted issues it found all 15 hidden relationships and all 13 problems, with zero false alarms.

Case notes

Distiller is an OpenAI-compatible gateway. It logs a company's repeated LLM calls, fine-tunes small open models with LoRA to imitate the big model on those tasks, tests them quietly in shadow mode, then routes the requests it's confident about to the cheap model, falling back to the big one when it isn't.

Status: the gateway skeleton and CLI work. Training and routing are in development.
